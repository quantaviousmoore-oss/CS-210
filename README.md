# Summarize the project and what problem it was solving.
he program allows users to enter an initial investment amount, a monthly deposit, an annual interest rate, and the number of years they plan to invest. It then calculates and displays how their investment could grow over time, both with and without additional monthly deposits. The problem it was solving was helping users visualize how consistent saving and compound interest can increase their money over the long term. The project also provided experience with C++ programming, object-oriented design, calculations, and displaying financial information in an organized format.
